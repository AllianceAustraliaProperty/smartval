In assessing the current market value of the subject premises, the two main Valuation methods were adopted which are Direct Comparison Approach and Analysis by Capitalisation Rate.

The preferred valuation approach for a block of units property of this type is to consider the valuation of the property under the Direct Comparison Approach. This involves the analysis of the sale of similar properties to determine per unit value which is applied to the number of units to determine the market value.

The secondary or check method is called Analysis by Capitalisation Rate, and this entails determining a Net Market Rental for the property and then capitalizing this potential income stream at an appropriate Capitalisation Rate.

In completing our valuation of the subject property, we have considered relevant general economic factors and have investigated recent sales transactions of comparable properties that have sold in the local market.

Our secondary method of assessment has been the Capitalisation Approach which is a traditional investment basis whereby the estimated net annual income is capitalised at an appropriate yield derived from market-based sales evidence.
